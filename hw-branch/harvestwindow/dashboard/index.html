Model and assumptions

How the recommendation works

External season and validation readiness

The public 2023 pre-trial archive was inspected. It contains 240 destructive plant records and 18,721 climate records. Its combined measurement sheet includes red-fruit count and total fruit-cluster weight, but no separate red-fruit fresh-weight label. Cross-season validation of this planner's weight-fraction target remains pending.

2023 pre-trial source

Forecast ripening

Fit a ripening curve to accumulated warmth in five completed greenhouse rooms. Shift it for the held-out room once three consecutive days of red canopy pixels are visible. Use warmth when that signal is missing.

Compare harvest days

Vary the ripening curve, warmth, daily costs and sale price. Use an 85% sellable-pot target and the earliest date within €0.005/m²/day of the scenario optimum. Scenario ranges describe sensitivity to these assumptions.

Sample when timing is disputed

Ask for a sample when some scenarios choose today and others choose a later date or have no eligible date. Update ripeness and fruit weight after sampling. If the decision remains disputed, reassess tomorrow.

One crop cycle at one site. Training uses the other rooms’ completed records. The held-out room only contributes data available by its cutoff. Fruit-weight share approximates the fruit-count eligibility rule. Prices, pot variation, future climate, and costs are scenario inputs. Harvesting on an alternate date has no observed profit outcome in this dataset.
